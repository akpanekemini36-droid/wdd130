My country: Flag

Nigeria

Nigeria is a country in west Africa. It has population of over 200 million people. Here is her flag. Designed in 1959 by Michael Taiwo Akinkunmi, the Nigerian flag features three vertical stripes of green and white. The two outer green bands symbolize lush vegetation, abundant natural wealth, and agricultural heritage. The central white band represents a steadfast desire for enduring peace, unity, and harmony across the entire country.
