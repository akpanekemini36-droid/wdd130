My favorite city

My favorite city is Uyo. Uyo is the serene and well-planned capital of Akwa Ibom State, celebrated across Nigeria for its exceptionally clean streets, lush greenery, and calm urban rhythm. It seamlessly blends deep-rooted Ibibio heritage and world-renowned cuisine like Afang and Edikang Ikong soup with modern landmarks like the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium and Ibom Icon Hotel. Today, the city stands out as one of the Niger Delta’s most peaceful and rapidly growing cultural and commercial hubs.
